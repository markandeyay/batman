# Batman demo video: voiceover script

Voice: ElevenLabs "Adam" (voice ID 6FiCmD8eY5VyjOdG5Zjk). One clip per scene, files in `public/vo/sNN.mp3`.
Scene lengths in the video follow the clip lengths (see `src/durations.json`).

| # | Scene | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hook: first date tonight | Tonight, you're meeting someone from a dating app for the first time. Your friends think it'll be fine. But nobody actually knows where you are. |
| 02 | Problem: panic buttons | Safety apps only work if you open them. And reaching for a panic button in front of a stranger is the last thing you want to do. |
| 03 | Meet Batman | Meet Batman. A safety buddy that lives in iMessage. There's no app. You just text it. |
| 04 | Sign up and backup contact | Sign up, and you get your own Batman number. Setup takes about a minute. Share a contact card to pick your backup, and they opt in before they ever hear from Batman. |
| 05 | Code words | Then pick two secret code words. An exit word gets you an excuse to leave. An alert word quietly texts your backup. |
| 06 | Plan | Before you go, text your plan the way you'd text a friend. Batman pulls out where and when, and you reply GO to lock it in. |
| 07 | Watch quietly | Share your location through Find My. Batman sees you arrive, tells you to have fun, and goes quiet. |
| 08 | Triggers | It only speaks up when something looks off. You stay past your end time, leave unexpectedly, speed off in a car, or your location goes dark. |
| 09 | Escalation ladder | First, a casual text. Reply all good, and it backs off. Go silent, and it nudges you again. Still nothing? Your backup gets an alert with your plan, your last location, and exactly why. |
| 10 | Code words in action | Feeling uneasy mid date? Text your exit word, and Batman texts back like a friend who needs you right now. Text your alert word, and your backup is notified, while your screen just says haha, okay. |
| 11 | Trust | Claude reads the conversation, but it never decides when to escalate. Fixed rules and timers do. Code words are matched before any AI sees them. |
| 12 | Close | Text home when you're back, and the plan closes. Batman. Someone always has your back. Try it at try batman dot vercel dot app. |
